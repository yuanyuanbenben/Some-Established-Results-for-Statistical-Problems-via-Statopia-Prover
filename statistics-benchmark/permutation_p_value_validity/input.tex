\begin{theorem}[Finite Permutation P-value Validity]
Let $N\ge 1$ and let $T_1,\ldots,T_N\in\mathbb R$ be the statistic values
over a finite permutation orbit. For each index $i$, define the upper-tail
permutation count
\[
C_i=\#\{j: T_j\ge T_i\}.
\]
The permutation p-value at index $i$ is $p_i=C_i/N$. Then, for every integer
$k$ with $0\le k\le N$,
\[
\#\{i:C_i\le k\}\le k.
\]
Equivalently, under the uniform distribution on the orbit,
\[
\mathbb P(p_I\le k/N)\le k/N .
\]
\end{theorem}
