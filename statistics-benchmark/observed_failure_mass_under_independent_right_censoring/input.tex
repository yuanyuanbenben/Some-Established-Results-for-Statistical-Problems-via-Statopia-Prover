\begin{theorem}[Observed failure mass under independent right censoring]
Let $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ be a probability space and let $m\ge1$.
Let $T,C:\Omega\to\mathbb N$ be measurable and satisfy
$1\le T(\omega)\le m$ and $1\le C(\omega)\le m$ for every $\omega$.
Assume that the failure time $T$ and censoring time $C$ are independent
under $P$.  Then, for every integer $u$ with $1\le u\le m$,
\[
 P\{\min(T,C)=u,\ T\le C\}
 =P\{T=u\}\,P\{u\le C\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
