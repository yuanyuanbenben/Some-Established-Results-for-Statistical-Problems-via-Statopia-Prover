\begin{theorem}[Gumbel Approximation to an Exponential Maximum]
Let $(\Omega,\mathcal A,P)$ be a probability space and let
$n\in\mathbb N$ with $n\ge1$.  Let
$X_0,\ldots,X_{n-1}:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ be measurable and independent,
with each $X_i$ having the exponential distribution of rate $1$.  Define
$i_0=0\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, and let $h_{\mathrm{ne}}$ be the
nonemptiness witness for $\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$ supplied by $i_0$.  Define
\[
  M(\omega)=\max_{0\le i<n}X_i(\omega).
\]
Then
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathbb R}
 \left|
 P\{M-\log n\le x\}
 -\exp\{-\exp(-x)\}
 \right|
 \le\frac1n.
\]
\end{theorem}
