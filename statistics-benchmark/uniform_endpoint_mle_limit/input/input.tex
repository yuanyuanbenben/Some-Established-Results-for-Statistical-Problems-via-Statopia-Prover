\begin{theorem}[Limit law of the uniform endpoint MLE]
Let $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ be a probability space and let $\theta>0$.
Let $(X_i)_{i\in\mathbb N}$ be measurable, mutually independent real-valued
random variables, each uniformly distributed on the open interval
$(0,\theta)$. Let $Z:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ be measurable and have the
exponential distribution with rate $\theta^{-1}$. For $r\in\mathbb N$,
define
\[
 M_r(\omega)=\max_{0\leq i\leq r}X_i(\omega).
\]
Then
\[
 (r+1)(\theta-M_r)\ \xrightarrow{\mathcal D}\ Z
 \quad\text{under }P\text{ as }r\to\infty.
\]
\end{theorem}
