\begin{theorem}[Negative Binomial Waiting-Time Mass Formula]
Let $0<p<1$, and let $r,m\in\mathbb{N}$ satisfy $1\le r\le m$. Consider
independent Bernoulli trials with success probability $p$. Let $T_r$ be the
trial index on which the $r$-th success occurs. Then
\[
\mathbb P(T_r=m)
=
\binom{m-1}{r-1}p^r(1-p)^{m-r}.
\]
\end{theorem}
