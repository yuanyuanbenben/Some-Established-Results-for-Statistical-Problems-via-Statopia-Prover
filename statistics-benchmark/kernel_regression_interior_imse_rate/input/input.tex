\begin{theorem}[Interior integrated risk rate for a product-kernel estimator]
Let $d,n\ge1$, let $\alpha>0$, and let $f_0:[0,1]^d\to\mathbb R$ be bounded
and $\alpha$-Hölder.  Let $X_0,\ldots,X_{n-1}$ have common uniform law on
the cube, and let $Y_i=f_0(X_i)+\xi_i$, where the regression noises are
centered and satisfy a common sub-exponential bound.  Let $K_h$ be a product
kernel satisfying the usual boundedness, Hölder, normalization, energy, and
measurability conditions.  Write
\[
  \widehat f_h(x)=\frac1n\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}K_h(x,X_i)Y_i,
  \qquad h=n^{-1/(2\alpha+d)}.
\]
For the interior band $I_h=[h,1-h]^d$, let $\mu_I$ be a finite measure with
$\mu_I(I_h)\le1$.  Assume the standard population-smoother identity, the
kernel $L^2$ energy calculation, and the centered fluctuation bound, with all
integrability obligations holding.  Then there is a constant $C>0$, depending
only on the uniform bounds in these assumptions, such that
\[
  \int_{I_h}\mathbb E\bigl(\widehat f_h(x)-f_0(x)\bigr)^2\,d\mu_I(x)
  \le C n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+d)}.
\]
\end{theorem}
