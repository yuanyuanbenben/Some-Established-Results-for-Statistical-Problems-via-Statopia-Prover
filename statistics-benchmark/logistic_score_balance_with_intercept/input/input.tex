\begin{theorem}[Logistic score balance with an intercept]
Let $n,d\ge1$, let $x_i\in\mathbb R^d$ and $y_i\in\{0,1\}$ for
$i=1,\ldots,n$, and let $\widehat a\in\mathbb R$ and
$\widehat b\in\mathbb R^d$.  Assume that $(\widehat a,\widehat b)$ is a
global maximizer of the logistic log-likelihood, in the explicit sense that
for every $a\in\mathbb R$ and $b\in\mathbb R^d$,
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^n\left[
   y_i(\widehat a+x_i^\top\widehat b)
   -\log\!\left(1+e^{\widehat a+x_i^\top\widehat b}\right)
 \right]
 \ge
 \sum_{i=1}^n\left[
   y_i(a+x_i^\top b)-\log\!\left(1+e^{a+x_i^\top b}\right)
 \right].
\]
Then the fitted probabilities balance the observed number of successes:
\[
  \sum_{i=1}^n\frac{1}{1+e^{-\widehat a-x_i^\top\widehat b}}
  =\sum_{i=1}^n y_i.
\]
\end{theorem}
