\begin{theorem}[Leading functional PCA perturbation bound]
Let $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ be a probability space and let $H$ be a
complete, second-countable real inner-product space.  Let
$X:\Omega\to H$ be strongly measurable, satisfy
\[
  \int_\Omega X(\omega)\,dP(\omega)=0,
\]
and have integrable squared norm.  Let $n\ge1$ and let
$x_1,\ldots,x_n\in H$.  Let $C,\widehat C:H\to H$ be continuous linear
maps satisfying, for every $h\in H$,
\[
  Ch=\int_\Omega\langle X(\omega),h\rangle X(\omega)\,dP(\omega),
  \qquad
  \widehat Ch=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\langle x_i,h\rangle x_i.
\]
Let $\phi,\widehat\phi\in H$ and
$\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\widehat\lambda\in\mathbb R$.  Assume
\[
  \|\phi\|=\|\widehat\phi\|=1,
  \qquad C\phi=\lambda_1\phi,
  \qquad \lambda_2<\lambda_1,
\]
and assume that every unit vector $h\in H$ orthogonal to $\phi$ satisfies
\[
  \langle Ch,h\rangle\le\lambda_2.
\]
Assume also
\[
  \widehat C\widehat\phi=\widehat\lambda\widehat\phi
\]
and that every unit vector $h\in H$ satisfies
\[
  \langle\widehat Ch,h\rangle\le\widehat\lambda.
\]
Define the eigengap $\delta=\lambda_1-\lambda_2$ and the operator-norm
error $\varepsilon=\|\widehat C-C\|$.  If
$\varepsilon\le\delta/2$, then
\[
  \sqrt{1-\langle\widehat\phi,\phi\rangle^2}
  \le\frac{\varepsilon}{\delta-\varepsilon}.
\]
\end{theorem}
